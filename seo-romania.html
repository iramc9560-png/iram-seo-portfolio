Why a Romania-specific plan?

Romanian searchers type with and without diacritics (ă, â, î, ș, ț), and local competitors, .ro domains and directories differ from the UK or USA. A plan built for this market avoids copying a UK or US strategy.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do SEO in the Romanian language?

I plan keyword research and content structure around Romanian search terms. For final Romanian copy, I recommend a native-speaking editor to keep the tone natural.

Do I need a .ro domain?

Not always, but a .ro domain or clear Romania targeting can help local relevance. The right setup depends on your business and I cover it in the audit.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most sites see measurable movement within 8 to 12 weeks of technical and on-page fixes, with stronger growth over six months or more.
