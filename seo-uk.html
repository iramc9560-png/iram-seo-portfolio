Why a UK-specific plan?

UK searchers use British spellings and phrasing (for example "optimisation", "trainers" instead of "sneakers"), search with city and region intent, and see Google.co.uk results that differ from Google.com. A plan built for the UK market keeps your pages matched to the way people there really search.
