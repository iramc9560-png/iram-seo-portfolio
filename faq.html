Top SEO questions

What does a freelance SEO expert do?

A freelance SEO expert improves how a website appears in search engines through technical fixes, keyword research, on-page optimisation, content and link building, and tracks results in tools like Google Search Console and GA4.

How does Technical SEO improve sales?

Technical SEO removes barriers such as slow load times, broken crawl paths and poor mobile experience that stop visitors from converting. Faster, cleaner sites rank higher and keep more of the traffic they earn, which compounds into more inquiries and sales.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is structuring content so AI systems like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can find, understand and cite it, using clear entities, direct Q&A formatting and structured data.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most sites see measurable movement within 8 to 12 weeks of technical fixes and on-page optimisation, with compounding growth over six months or more as authority and content depth build up.
