Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to the questions clients ask most — the kind AI search tools look for.

How does Technical SEO improve sales?

Technical SEO removes the barriers — slow load times, broken crawl paths, poor mobile experience — that stop otherwise-interested visitors from converting. Faster, cleaner, well-structured sites rank higher and keep more of the traffic they earn, which directly compounds into more inquiries and sales.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of structuring content so AI systems like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews can accurately find, understand and cite it when answering user questions — using clear entities, direct Q&A formatting and well-marked structured data (schema).

Do you work with international clients?

Yes — I build concept audits and market-entry keyword strategies for businesses targeting the UK, Europe and Romania, mapping local search behaviour and language differences into a phased, white-label SEO roadmap.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most sites see measurable movement within 8–12 weeks of technical fixes and on-page optimization, with compounding growth over 6+ months as authority and content depth build up.
